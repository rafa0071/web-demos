Questions? Answers.

Do I need to change my email provider?

No. Relaydesk connects to Gmail, Outlook or any IMAP inbox. Customers keep writing to the same address.

Is the AI sending emails on its own?

Never. AI only drafts a suggestion. A teammate always reviews, edits and clicks send.

Can I import my old conversations?

Yes, the last 12 months of email are imported automatically when you connect your inbox.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose a plan or drop to Starter. We never charge without asking first.
